# Pocket Drone

<ItemImage id="cyberfarm:pocket_drone" scale="2" float="left" />

A tiny drone that rides folded on your [Utility Belt](utility_belt.md) and **picks up the drops around
you**. It is a small cousin of the Builder's mini drones.

Make it at the [Trinket Table](trinket_table.md), then put it in the drone holder of your belt (press **B**
to open it). It works only from the belt.

## Using it

Press **K** to send it out. It opens its arms and flies up to wait over your shoulder. Press **K** again to
call it back.

- When an item lies on the ground within **8 blocks**, it flies over, picks it up and brings it to your
  inventory, one drop at a time.
- Items you threw away yourself are left alone for **5 seconds**.
- Anything you have no room for stays on the ground.
- Call it back mid-errand, and what it holds comes straight to you.

## Power

The drone has a small battery of its own. The [Energy Sphere](energy_sphere.md) on your belt keeps it
topped up, whether it flies or not.

| Pocket Drone | |
| --- | --- |
| Battery | 20,000 FE |
| Use in flight | 1 FE/t |
| Refill from the sphere | up to 10 FE/t |
| Pickup range | 8 blocks |

When its battery runs out, it switches off and folds back on your belt. With an empty battery, **K** does not send it
out. A [Human Dock](human_dock.md) charges it too.
