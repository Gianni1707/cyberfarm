# Cyber Frame

<BlockImage id="cyberfarm:cyber_frame" scale="4" float="left" />

The **Cyber Frame Block** is CyberFarm's structural block. Many machines need one or more Cyber
Frames beside them before they work, and the big chambers are built out of them. Craft a stack
early: you will use a lot of them.

<br clear="all" />

## Making it

4 Iron Ingots and 4 Copper Ingots make **8** Cyber Frames. Iron and copper from other mods work too.

<RecipeFor id="cyber_frame" />

## Small machines

Most CyberFarm machines are small multiblocks: the machine plus a few Cyber Frames next to it.

1. Place the machine.
2. Right-click it alone: drones fly out and show where each frame goes.
3. Place the frames there. The machine assembles itself and takes the frames into its shape.

A machine works only once assembled. Break one of its frames and it folds back. Break the machine
and its frames stay behind as plain Cyber Frames.

## Big chambers

Two machines sit in a **3x3x4 chamber** of Cyber Frames and <ItemLink id="containment_glass" />.
Each chamber takes **25** Cyber Frames: a floor, the four corners and a roof.

| Chamber | Ports that can replace a frame |
| --- | --- |
| [Methane Extractor](methane_extractor.md) | Acid Inlet, Methane Outlet |
| [Crystallizer](crystallizer.md) | Crystallizer Port |

The [Multiblock Projector](multiblock_projector.md) shows both chambers in the world before you build them.

## Tips

- A Cyber Frame that is already part of a finished chamber does not count for a small machine.
- Once a small machine is built, its frames take cables, pipes and hoppers just like the machine block: connect
  wherever is handiest.
