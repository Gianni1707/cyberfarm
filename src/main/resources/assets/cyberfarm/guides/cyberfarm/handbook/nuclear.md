# Nuclear Power

The most powerful energy in CyberFarm, and the most demanding. A **Fission Reactor** shaped like a
cooling tower burns uranium **Fuel Rods**; its core heats water into **Steam**, and **Steam Turbines**
turn the steam into FE, just like a real power plant.

## The power plant

1. Mine [uranium](uranium.md) deep underground and craft **Fuel Rods**.
2. Build the [Fission Reactor](fission_reactor.md) tower.
3. Feed it **water** with a [Water Pump](water_pump.md) and [Gas Pipes](gas_pipe.md).
4. Send the steam to [Steam Turbines](steam_turbine.md) and the FE into [Energy Conduits](energy_conduit.md).
5. To run the reactor harder, cool it better with a [Coolant Mixer](coolant_mixer.md).

## Safety first

A reactor keeps burning as long as its power is on, **whether or not it is cooled**. If the heat it
makes is more than the water and coolant can carry away, the core gets hotter. At **80%** an alarm
sounds; at **100%** the core **melts down** and explodes. Watch the temperature bar, and turn the
power down (or off) when something goes wrong.
