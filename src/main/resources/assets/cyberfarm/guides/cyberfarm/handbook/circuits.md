# Circuits

Every machine, module and frame runs on a circuit. Each tier has its own, and each one is made from
the circuit before it.

  <ItemIcon id="basic_circuit" />
  <ItemIcon id="advanced_circuit" />

## The three circuits

| Circuit | Tier | Made in | Needs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic Circuit | 1 | Crafting table or Fabricator | Copper, redstone, gold nugget |
| Advanced Circuit | 2 | Fabricator only | 2 Basic Circuits, gold ingot, **Acid** |
| Elite Circuit | 3 | Fabricator only | Advanced Circuit, diamond, uranium ingot, **Acid** |

## By hand

Your first **Basic Circuits** come from a crafting table: 2 Copper Ingots, 2 Redstone and
1 Gold Nugget make **one** circuit. It is slow and costly on purpose: build a
<ItemLink id="fabricator" /> as soon as you can. It makes **two** Basic Circuits from 1 Copper Ingot,
2 Redstone and 1 Gold Nugget.

<RecipeFor id="basic_circuit" />

## In the Fabricator

The Advanced and Elite Circuits are etched with **Acid** in the [Fabricator](fabricator.md), which
lists every recipe with its time.

- The Acid comes from an [Acid Synthesizer](acid_synthesizer.md), made from Nether sulfur.
- The uranium comes from [uranium ore](uranium.md).
