# Fission Reactor

The Fission Reactor is the strongest power source in CyberFarm. It burns uranium
[Fuel Rods](fuel_rod.md) to boil water into **Steam**, and [Steam Turbines](steam_turbine.md) turn
that steam into FE.

It is a cooling tower in **three sizes**, built from <ItemLink id="reactor_casing" />s, a few
<ItemLink id="reactor_port" />s and one <ItemLink id="reactor_controller" />. The scene shows the
Small tower.

## Building the tower

Bigger towers burn more rods and reach more power:

| Size | Base x height | Fuel Rods burned | Max power |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 5x5 x 7 | 2 | 200% |
| Medium | 7x7 x 11 | 4 | 350% |
| Large | 9x9 x 15 | 8 | 500% |

Every tower is a full square floor, then hollow square rings: wide at the bottom and the rim,
narrow at the waist. Ring widths in blocks, layer by layer from the bottom:

| Size | Floor | Rings above it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 5x5 | 5, 5, 3, 3, 3, 5 |
| Medium | 7x7 | 7, 7, 7, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 7, 7 |
| Large | 9x9 | 9, 9, 9, 9, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7, 9, 9, 9 |

1. Build the floor and the rings out of <ItemLink id="reactor_casing" />s.
2. Leave everything **inside** the rings **empty**. Blocks outside a narrower ring do not matter.
3. Put the <ItemLink id="reactor_controller" /> **in the middle of one side of layer 2** (the first
   ring above the floor), facing out. It finds the size by itself, trying the largest first.
4. Swap shell blocks for <ItemLink id="reactor_port" />s where pipes, cables or hoppers connect.
   Two are enough: one for water in, one for steam out. Ports go only **in the middle of the four
   sides** (see below), or in the floor.

A Large tower moves more water and steam than basic [Liquid Pipes](liquid_pipe.md) and
[Gas Pipes](gas_pipe.md) carry (1,000 mB/t): use **Advanced** pipes at its water and steam ports.

**Help with the layout**: a [Multiblock Projector](multiblock_projector.md) shows any size right
where you want to build it. You can also **right-click the unfinished controller**: drones fly out,
each projecting a group of the blocks still to place, and an amber X marks a block in the way. They
start from the size you have built the most of (Small if none). Each further click shows the next
size; after Large they come back to Small.

When the tower is complete it powers up with a deep chime and **turns into a real cooling tower**:
the blocky casings become one faceted, octagonal shell of enamel panels with glowing mint bands. The
controller becomes a **panel** on the surface and every port a glowing **hatch**, right where a pipe
or cable enters the tower. **Right-click any block of the tower** to open the reactor.

## Ports

Reactor Ports are where the outside world connects:

| In | Out |
| --- | --- |
| Fuel Rods (hoppers, item pipes of other mods) | Depleted Fuel Rods |
| Water and [Coolant](coolant.md) ([Liquid Pipes](liquid_pipe.md), buckets) | Steam, pushed out by itself ([Gas Pipes](gas_pipe.md)) |

A port on a wall connects only **outwards**, on the face where its hatch is: a pipe running past it above
or below does not plug in. A port in the floor connects only **from below**.

**Where ports go.** On the walls, only in the middle of each side: the tower is drawn with diagonal
faces towards its corners, and a pipe could not reach a port there. On a ring 3 or 5 blocks wide that is
the centre block of each side; on rings 7 or 9 wide, the centre block and the one on each side of it.
A port nearer a corner leaves the tower **incomplete**, and the projector marks it as a block in the
way. Inside the floor, ports go anywhere and are reached from below.

A port in a wall shared by two towers works for the tower that took it first.

## The reactor GUI

- **Left**: the **water** tank and the **coolant** tank (16,000 mB each). Under the coolant tank:
  the **Dump coolant** button.
- **Title**: the size of the tower.
- **Middle**: 8 Fuel Rod slots (the ones a smaller tower cannot burn are dimmed and take no rods, by hand or by pipe), the core (one bar
  per rod, emerald while it burns) and 4 waste slots for Depleted Fuel Rods. Below them, the
  **temperature** bar with its red alarm mark, and a status line.
- **Bottom**: the **power** buttons, **-** and **+**.
- **Right**: the **steam** tank (16,000 mB). Under it: the **Dump excess steam** toggle.

Hover the temperature bar to see the heat the core makes, how much it can cool and the steam it
makes, all per tick. Hover the power buttons to see the tower's maximum.

### Power

Power goes from **Off** up to the tower's maximum, in **steps of 50%**. A new reactor starts **Off**:
insert rods, connect water, then raise the power. Rods only burn while the power is above Off.

### Dump coolant

The coolant tank holds **one kind** at a time: Coolant or Cryo Coolant. To switch:

1. Set the power to **Off**.
2. Click the small button **under the coolant tank**. The whole tank is poured away and lost.
3. Pipe in the new coolant.

The button only works while the power is Off; it lights amber when you can use it. With the tank
empty the tower cools with plain water, so lower the power before you raise it again.

### Dump excess steam

A toggle **under the steam tank**, off at first. While it is **on** (lit), every tick the steam
above **8,000 mB** (half the steam tank) is vented into the air and **lost**. The turbines still
get their steam first.

Turn it on when your turbines cannot take all the steam: the steam tank never fills up, so the
reactor keeps cooling instead of overheating. Vented steam makes no FE.

## Heat and cooling

- Each burning rod makes **50 heat per tick at 100% power**. 4 rods at 250% make 500 heat per tick.
- Each tick some of that heat goes into the **water**: 1 mB of water takes 1 heat and leaves as
  1 mB of **Steam**. More power means more steam and more FE.
- How much heat can leave each tick depends on the tower size and on the **primary loop**: the
  fluid in the coolant tank. With the tank empty it is plain water.

| Size | Water | Coolant (2.5x) | Cryo Coolant (6x) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | 150 | 375 | 900 |
| Medium | 250 | 625 | 1,500 |
| Large | 400 | 1,000 | 2,400 |

Heat per tick the tower can move. Coolants wear out while they work: see [Coolant](coolant.md).

Heat can only leave when there is **water** in the water tank and **room** in the steam tank.

## What each tower produces

The highest power that does **not** overheat, with every rod slot of the tower filled. The steam
made each tick equals the water used. A [Water Pump](water_pump.md) gives 500 mB/t.

| Tower | Primary loop | Safe power | Steam = water (mB/t) | Water Pumps | Rod life |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small | Water | 150% | 150 | 1 | 26 min 40 s |
| Small | Coolant or Cryo | 200% (max) | 200 | 1 | 20 min |
| Medium | Water | 100% | 200 | 1 | 40 min |
| Medium | Coolant | 300% | 600 | 2 | 13 min 20 s |
| Medium | Cryo Coolant | 350% (max) | 700 | 2 | 11 min 26 s |
| Large | Water | 100% | 400 | 1 | 40 min |
| Large | Coolant | 250% | 1,000 | 2 | 16 min |
| Large | Cryo Coolant | 500% (max) | 2,000 | 4 | 8 min |

Cryo Coolant gains nothing in a Small tower: Coolant already cools it at full power.

What that steam gives in FE/t. Each cell reads **turbines needed → FE/t**, using only turbines of
that size:

| Tower and loop | Steam (mB/t) | Small turbines | Medium turbines | Large turbines |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Small, Water | 150 | 1 → 1,500 | 1 → 2,400 | 1 → 3,750 |
| Small, Coolant or Cryo | 200 | 1 → 2,000 | 1 → 3,200 | 1 → 5,000 |
| Medium, Water | 200 | 1 → 2,000 | 1 → 3,200 | 1 → 5,000 |
| Medium, Coolant | 600 | 3 → 6,000 | 1 → 9,600 | 1 → 15,000 |
| Medium, Cryo | 700 | 4 → 7,000 | 2 → 11,200 | 1 → 17,500 |
| Large, Water | 400 | 2 → 4,000 | 1 → 6,400 | 1 → 10,000 |
| Large, Coolant | 1,000 | 5 → 10,000 | 2 → 16,000 | 1 → 25,000 |
| Large, Cryo | 2,000 | 10 → 20,000 | 4 → 32,000 | 2 → 50,000 |

**The turbines must take all the steam.** If they cannot, the steam tank fills, the water has
nowhere to go and the core overheats, unless **Dump excess steam** is on. A turbine also stops when
its FE has nowhere to go.

## Overheating and meltdown

Heat that cannot leave stays in the core, and the **temperature** rises.

- Above **80%** an **alarm** rings and the GUI flashes *OVERHEATING!*. Lower the power, add water or
  coolant, or make room for the steam (more turbines, or Dump excess steam).
- At **100%** the core **melts down**: the rods are destroyed, the tower **explodes** and the area
  around it becomes heavily [radioactive](radiation.md) for a long time. The blast strength and
  whether it breaks blocks are set in the common config (*Meltdown Explosion Power*, *Meltdown
  Destroys Blocks*).

How fast it goes depends on how much heat is left over. One power step too high can take minutes. A
Large tower at 500% that runs out of water or Cryo Coolant melts down in about **6 seconds**.

An idle core cools down slowly by itself.

**No escape**: breaking the controller or any block of the tower while the temperature is above 80%
does not save you. The core melts down anyway. Set the power to Off and let it cool first.

## Troubleshooting

| Status or problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| *Tower incomplete* | Check the layout; the drones or a Multiblock Projector show what is missing. |
| *Off: raise the power* | Press **+**. |
| *Ready: insert Fuel Rods* | Put Fuel Rods in the slots the tower uses. |
| Steam tank full | Add turbines, give their FE somewhere to go, or turn on Dump excess steam. |
| Temperature climbing | Lower the power, check the water supply and the coolant level. |
| Waste slots full | The spent rods wait in the core and it **leaks radiation**. Pull the waste out with a hopper under a Reactor Port in the floor, or an item pipe on a wall port and treat it in a [Waste Reprocessor](waste_reprocessor.md). |
| Coolant running out | Cooling falls to the plain water level. Keep a [Coolant Mixer](coolant_mixer.md) piped in. |
