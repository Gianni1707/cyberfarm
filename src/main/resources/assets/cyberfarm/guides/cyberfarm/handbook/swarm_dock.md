# Builder Swarm Dock

<BlockImage id="cyberfarm:swarm_dock" scale="3" float="left" />

A dock for up to **four Mini Drones**: drones built on a [Mini Drone Frame](drone_frames.md).
Use it to raise a structure with a swarm of [Builder](modules.md) drones, or to guard an area with
[Bomb](modules.md) drones.

<br clear="all" />

## Placing and powering

It works like a [Drone Dock](drone_dock.md): power it with a generator or cables from any side,
and it charges the drones, stores what they bring home in its 9 slots and holds their settings.

- **Right-click** the swarm dock with a Mini Drone to deploy it. It takes up to four, each
  parked on its own corner of the pad.
- Bigger drones do not fit. Mini Drones do not fit an ordinary Drone Dock.
- The four drones **share** its work area, transport route, build and stock.
- Set its work area with the [Survey Tool](survey_tool.md), as for any dock.

<RecipeFor id="swarm_dock" />

## Building with a swarm

1. Pick a structure in the [Multiblock Projector](multiblock_projector.md) and lock it in place.
2. **Right-click the swarm dock** with the projector to link the build.
3. Put the blocks in the dock.

Four Builder Mini Drones then raise it together and split its blocks between them. See the
[Builder Module](modules.md) for what they clear and how they build.

## The screen

The screen works like a Drone Dock's, and shows the swarm as one:

- the busiest drone's job;
- their batteries added up;
- how many drones are on the dock.

## Good to know

- Each of the four landing rings **lights up** while a Mini Drone stands on it.
- An [Anchor Addon](addons.md) works here too, for the anchored Mini Drones.
- The [Hologram Table](hologram_table.md) shows the swarm dock as a pillar of light, like any dock.
