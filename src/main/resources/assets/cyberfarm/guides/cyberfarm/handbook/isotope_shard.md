# Isotope Shard

<ItemImage id="cyberfarm:isotope_shard" scale="2" float="left" />

An Isotope Shard is a rare sliver of isotope recovered from spent fuel. It is the seed of the
**Fusion Crystal** at the heart of every [Mech Core](mech_core.md).

<br clear="all" />

- **Get it**: the [Waste Reprocessor](waste_reprocessor.md) recovers one from **1 Depleted Fuel Rod
  in 4**.
- **Use it**: the Crystallizer grows it into a Fusion Crystal. See [Mech Core](mech_core.md).
