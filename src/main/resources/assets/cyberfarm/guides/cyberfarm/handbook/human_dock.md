# Human Dock

<BlockImage id="cyberfarm:human_dock" scale="3" float="left" />

The [Drone Dock](drone_dock.md)'s sibling, for you. Step onto its pad and it **charges everything you
carry at once**: the [Energy Sphere](energy_sphere.md) and the [Pocket Drone](pocket_drone.md) on your
belt, and every item in your inventory that holds FE, from any mod.

## Building it

1. Place the Human Dock.
2. Place a <ItemLink id="cyber_frame" /> **on top** of it.

The frame hides and the arch rises behind the pad like a tripod's legs, a clamp snapping shut at each
joint. A display slides out of its top showing the dock's charge.

<RecipeFor id="human_dock" />

## Powering it

It takes FE from cables, or from a [Battery Bank](battery_bank.md) beside it (with that side of the bank
set to Out).

## Using it

**Step onto the pad.** It charges, in this order:

1. the Energy Sphere on your belt;
2. the Pocket Drone on your belt;
3. every FE item in your inventory, armor and offhand, spare Energy Spheres included.

It has no screen: its lights show it working. Several players on the pad are each charged.

## Numbers

| --- | --- |
