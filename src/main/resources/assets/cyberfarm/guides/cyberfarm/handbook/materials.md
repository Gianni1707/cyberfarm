# Materials and Progression

CyberFarm grows in **three tiers**. Each tier is built from what the tier before it makes, so you
always know what to build next. This section covers the materials and the machines that make them.

## The three tiers

| Tier | Materials | Made with | Unlocks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | [Aluminum](aluminum.md), [Basic Circuits](circuits.md) | [Electrolyzer](electrolyzer.md), a crafting table or the [Fabricator](fabricator.md) | Basic and Mini Drone Frames, modules, addons, docks, the Assembler, Bio-Digester, Gas Turbine, Methane Extractor, Acid Synthesizer, Pyrolysis Oven, Water Pump |
| **2** | [Carbon Fiber](carbon_fiber.md), [Advanced Circuits](circuits.md) | [Pyrolysis Oven](pyrolysis_oven.md) (methane), Fabricator (acid) | Advanced Frames, Steam Engine, Coolant Mixer, Waste Reprocessor, Advanced cables, Resonance Core, Mech Hangar, Laser Turret, the dishes |
| **3** | [Elite Circuits](circuits.md) | Fabricator (acid, uranium) | Elite Frames, the Fission Reactor, the Steam Turbine, Elite cables, the Crystallizer, Range Module III and the mech |

## The path

1. **By hand**: mine **bauxite** and smelt it into alumina. Run a
   [Combustion Generator](combustion_generator.md) and an Electrolyzer for your first aluminum.
   Craft your first Basic Circuits at a crafting table.
2. **Gas**: the Advanced Circuits are etched with **Acid**, made from Nether **sulfur**. The Carbon
   Fiber is drawn out of **Methane** from a [Methane Extractor](methane_extractor.md). Both need
   the gas tier of [Generators](generators.md).
3. **Nuclear**: the Elite Circuits need **uranium**, and the [reactor](nuclear.md) needs Elite Circuits.

Like real drones, the frames start in light **aluminum** and move on to **carbon fiber**.

Most of these machines are small multiblocks: each needs one or more
[Cyber Frames](cyber_frame.md) beside it before it works.
