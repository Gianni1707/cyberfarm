# Drones

Drones are the heart of CyberFarm: modular flying workers that harvest, plant, dig, chop trees,
carry items and even give you a ride, then fly home to unload and recharge.

## From parts to a working drone

1. Pick a **frame** in [Drone Frames](drone_frames.md): it decides battery, speed and how many
   modules and addons fit.
2. Choose its jobs with [Function Modules](modules.md).
3. Tune it with [Addons](addons.md): speed, bigger loads, battery, Fortune or Silk Touch.
4. Put it all together in the [Drone Assembler](drone_assembler.md).
5. Give it a home: a powered [Drone Dock](drone_dock.md), with a work area marked by the Survey Tool.
6. Deploy it, change it and recover it as shown in [Using Drones](drones.md).
