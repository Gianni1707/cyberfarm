# Drones

Drones are the heart of CyberFarm: modular flying workers that do the farm's jobs for you. They
harvest, plant, dig, chop trees, tend animals, fish, guard, build, drain liquids, carry items and
even give you a ride, then fly home to unload and recharge.

## From parts to a working drone

1. Pick a **frame** in [Drone Frames](drone_frames.md). It decides the battery, the speed and how
   many modules and addons fit.
2. Choose its jobs with [Function Modules](modules.md).
3. Tune it with [Addons](addons.md): speed, bigger loads, battery, efficiency, Fortune, Silk Touch
   or chunk loading.
4. Put it all together in the [Drone Assembler](drone_assembler.md).
5. Give it a home: a powered [Drone Dock](drone_dock.md), or a [Builder Swarm Dock](swarm_dock.md)
   for Mini Drones.
6. Mark where it works with the [Survey Tool](survey_tool.md).
7. Deploy it, change it and recover it as shown in [Using Drones](drones.md).
8. Watch the whole fleet at work on a [Hologram Table](hologram_table.md).

Drones can also feed a village: see [Supply Terminal](supply_terminal.md) and
[The Tech Village](tech_village.md).
