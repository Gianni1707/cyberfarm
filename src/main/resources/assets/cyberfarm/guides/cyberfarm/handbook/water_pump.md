# Water Pump

<BlockImage id="cyberfarm:water_pump" scale="4" float="left" />

An endless supply of water for reactors and the Coolant Mixer. Place it touching **at least 2 water
source blocks** (the same rule as an infinite water source) and give it power.

<br clear="all" />

- Pumps **250 mB/t** with **20 FE/t** and pushes it into adjacent [Gas Pipes](gas_pipe.md) and machines.
- One pump feeds a reactor with 4 rods up to 125% power; add pumps for more.
- It has no GUI: **right-click** it to see what it is doing.
