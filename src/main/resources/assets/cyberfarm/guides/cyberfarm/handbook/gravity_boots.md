# Gravity Boots

<ItemImage id="cyberfarm:gravity_boots" scale="2" float="left" />

Boots that make you **hover over the ground**, following the terrain like a hovercraft. Steps and slabs
disappear under you, you glide over water and lava without touching them, and you take **no fall damage**.

Make them at the [Trinket Table](trinket_table.md) and wear them in the **Feet** slot. They run on the
[Energy Sphere](energy_sphere.md) in your [Utility Belt](utility_belt.md).

## Using them

- Press **H** to pick the hover height: **0.5**, **1** or **2 blocks**, or off. Each press moves to the next.
- **Sneak to land.**
- **Step assist**: a step up to a full block high rises under you as you reach it, smoothly, at any hover
  height. You never bump into a single block.
- Walking, sprinting and jumping work as on foot. You stop and turn with a touch of drift, as on a little ice.
- Rings of light leave your soles and sink to the ground under you.

Off a cliff, the field slows you down and catches you at your height above the ground below.

## Numbers

| Gravity Boots | |
| --- | --- |
| Heights | 0.5, 1 or 2 blocks |
| Speed while hovering | +40% |
| Power | 12 FE/t while you hover |
| Full Energy Sphere | about 35 minutes of hovering |

## Troubleshooting

They do not hover when:

- the Energy Sphere is empty, or missing from your belt;
- the height is set to off;
- you are flying in creative, gliding with an elytra, riding, sleeping or in spectator mode.
