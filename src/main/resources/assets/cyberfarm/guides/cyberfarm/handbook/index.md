# Drone Engineer's Handbook

<ItemImage id="cyberfarm:drone" scale="2" float="left" />

Welcome, engineer! **CyberFarm** lets you build modular drones that run your farms for you, and
the machines and power plants that keep them going. This page is a map of the handbook.

<br clear="all" />

## Good to know

- You build a drone from a **frame**, one or more **function modules** and optional **addons**.
  It lives on a **Drone Dock** and does the work while you do something more fun.
- Drones and machines run on **FE** (Forge Energy). Power from any other tech mod works too.
- Hold **G** over a CyberFarm item, in your inventory or in JEI, to open its page here.
- The info page (the **"i"** tab) of any CyberFarm machine gives a quick explanation.
- Using **JEI** or **REI**? Look up the uses of a machine to see what it takes, what it makes and
  its numbers. Look up the Drone Assembler to see what each frame tier can hold.
