# Drone Engineer's Handbook

<ItemImage id="cyberfarm:drone" scale="2" float="left" />

Welcome, engineer! **CyberFarm** lets you build modular drones that run your farms for you.
You assemble a drone from a **frame**, one or more **function modules** and optional **addons**, give it a
**Drone Dock** to live on, and it does the work while you do something more fun.

Drones and machines run on **FE** (Forge Energy), so they work with power from any other tech mod.

Using **JEI** or **REI**? Look up the Drone Assembler to see what each frame tier can hold, and
the uses of any machine to see what it takes, what it makes and its numbers: organics for the
Bio-Digester, mobs for the Methane Extractor, fuels for the turbines, rods and coolants for the
reactor and more. The info page (the "i" tab) of any CyberFarm machine gives a quick explanation.

<br clear="all" />

## Where to start

New here? Read [Getting Started](getting_started.md): your first drone, step by step.
Base too loud? A [Muffler](muffler.md) silences machines, drones and mobs around it.
Need water for your machines? A [Water Pump](water_pump.md) gives an endless supply.
Then the handbook has five sections:

### [Generators](generators.md)
Making FE: the [Combustion Generator](combustion_generator.md), then [Gas Power](gas_power.md)
with the [Bio-Digester](bio_digester.md), the [Methane Extractor](methane_extractor.md) and the
[Gas Turbine](gas_turbine.md).

### [Nuclear](nuclear.md)
The endgame power plant: [uranium](uranium.md), the [Fission Reactor](fission_reactor.md), the
[Coolant Mixer](coolant_mixer.md), the [Steam Turbine](steam_turbine.md), [waste and radiation](radiation.md),
the [Waste Reprocessor](waste_reprocessor.md) and the [Hazmat Suit and Geiger Counter](hazmat_suit.md).

The [Multiblock Projector](multiblock_projector.md) shows any of these structures in the world
before you build it.

### [Energy Storage](energy_storage.md)
Keeping power for later: the [Resonance Core](resonance_core.md), a battery you build bigger with a
shell of Resonance Cells, held steady by columns of Stabilizer Pylons that are also its way in and out.

### [Cables](cables.md)
Moving power and fluids: the [Energy Conduit](energy_conduit.md), the [Gas Pipe](gas_pipe.md) and the
[Liquid Pipe](liquid_pipe.md), plus [Liquid and Gas Tanks](fluid_tanks.md) to store fluids.

### [Drones](drone_systems.md)
Building and running drones: the [Drone Assembler](drone_assembler.md),
[Drone Frames](drone_frames.md), [Function Modules](modules.md), [Addons](addons.md),
the [Drone Dock](drone_dock.md) and [Using Drones](drones.md).
