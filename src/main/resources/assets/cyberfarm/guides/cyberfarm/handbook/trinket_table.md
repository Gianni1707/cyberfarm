# Trinket Table

<BlockImage id="cyberfarm:trinket_table" scale="3" float="left" />

The workstation where every [trinket](trinkets.md) and every Neural Chip program is made. Trinkets and
programs can be made **only here**. It needs no power.

## Building it

The table is a **2x2** multiblock standing upright.

1. Place the Trinket Table.
2. Place a <ItemLink id="cyber_frame" /> on its **right** (seen from the front).
3. Place a Cyber Frame **above the table**, and one **above that frame**.

The table opens up, and its two robotic arms are ready to work.

<RecipeFor id="trinket_table" />

## Using it

It works like a **crafting table**, but shapeless:

- Put **one item in each slot** of its 3x3 grid, in any order.
- Take what it makes. Its arms go to work for a moment.
- Ingredients needed twice take two slots.

## What it makes

| Result | Ingredients |
| --- | --- |
| [Utility Belt](utility_belt.md) | 4 Leather, 2 Aluminum Ingots, 2 String |
| [Energy Sphere](energy_sphere.md) | 4 Battery Cells, 1 Advanced Circuit, 2 Glass, 2 Aluminum Ingots |
| Advanced Energy Sphere | 1 Energy Sphere, 2 Advanced Circuits, 4 Battery Cells, 2 Carbon Fiber |
| Elite Energy Sphere | 1 Advanced Energy Sphere, 2 Elite Circuits, 2 Diamonds, 2 Carbon Fiber, 1 Eye of Ender |
| [Gravity Boots](gravity_boots.md) | 4 Carbon Fiber, 2 Rotors, 2 Phantom Membranes, 1 Advanced Circuit |
| [Neural Chip](neural_chip.md) | 2 Advanced Circuits, 2 Gold Ingots, 1 Ender Pearl, 1 Carbon Fiber, 1 Diamond |
| [Pocket Drone](pocket_drone.md) | 1 Mini Frame, 1 Drone Core, 1 Hopper, 2 Basic Circuits |
| Any program | 1 Basic Circuit, 2 Redstone Dust, plus the item below |

| Program | Extra item |
| --- | --- |
| Zoom | 1 Spyglass |
| Night Vision | 2 Golden Carrots |
| Machine Link | 1 Comparator |
| Geiger HUD | 1 Geiger Counter |
| Taxi Call | 1 Bell |
| Auto-Feeder | 1 Hay Bale |

Aluminum from any mod works. An upgraded Energy Sphere **keeps the charge** of the sphere you put in.

## Installing programs

Put a Neural Chip and the programs you want to add in the grid together, and nothing else. The chip comes
out with them installed. See [Neural Chip](neural_chip.md).
