# Utility Belt

<ItemImage id="cyberfarm:utility_belt" scale="2" float="left" />

A leather belt with a mint enamel stripe, worn in the **Belt** slot. It carries the
[Energy Sphere](energy_sphere.md) that powers all your [trinkets](trinkets.md), your building tools and
the [Pocket Drone](pocket_drone.md). Everything in it hangs on your belt, where you can see it.

Make it at the [Trinket Table](trinket_table.md).

## Using it

Press **B** to open the belt. It has **five holders**, one for each of these items. Under them you see
the charge of the sphere and of the drone.

| Holder | Key | What the key does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Energy Sphere](energy_sphere.md) | | Powers your trinkets, and charges the FE items you carry |
| <ItemLink id="multiblock_projector" /> | Y | Takes it in hand, or puts it back |
| <ItemLink id="survey_tool" /> | U | Takes it in hand, or puts it back |
| [Pocket Drone](pocket_drone.md) | K | Sends it out, or calls it back |
| <ItemLink id="cable_swapper" /> | I | Takes it in hand, or puts it back |

When a tool comes to your hand, what you held moves to a free slot of your inventory. If there is no room
for it, the tool stays on the belt.

To put a tool back, hold it and press its key again. This also works for a tool you hold that was never
on the belt, as long as its holder is empty.

## Charging from the belt

Once a second, the Energy Sphere on your belt charges **every item you carry that holds FE**, from any
mod. See [Energy Sphere](energy_sphere.md) for the rates.

## Tips

- Without a belt, or without a sphere in it, your trinkets have no power. Only the Zoom program works without it.
- You can change every key in Controls, under **CyberFarm Trinkets**. A trinket's tooltip shows the key it is bound to now.
